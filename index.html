[ Decision Logic ]

  • 1. One pooled logistic regression estimates, for every ETF, the probability of a gain of at least the threshold over the horizon. Its inputs are the ETFs' features ranked against each other on the same date.
  • 2. The ETFs are ranked by that probability. The top share of the ranking is [ Up ], all others are [ Not Up ].
  • 3. Model gate: if the lower bound of the 95% interval of the walk-forward validation AUC is not above the threshold, no ETF is [ Up ].
